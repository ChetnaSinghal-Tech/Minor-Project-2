Understanding Donation

Knowledge is the first step toward becoming a hero.

Whole Blood

The most common type. One donation can save up to three lives. Used for surgeries, trauma, and anemia.

  • Duration: 10-15 mins
  • Frequency: Every 90 days

Bone Marrow

Crucial for patients with leukemia or lymphoma. It replaces unhealthy blood-forming cells with healthy ones.

  • Need: Genetic match required
  • Impact: Cures blood cancers

PBSC (Stem Cells)

Peripheral Blood Stem Cell donation is a non-surgical process similar to donating plasma. Used for bone marrow transplants.

  • Process: Apheresis (Blood filtering)
  • Recovery: Usually within 24 hours
